Browser capture

Install the Eyentra screenshot extension.

Log in at https://eyentra.vercel.app, click the extension icon, drag over any visible browser section, and Eyentra will upload that crop to your account with a QR code and share password.

Install in Chrome or Edge

  1. Open https://eyentra.vercel.app/login and log in.
  2. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
  3. Enable Developer mode.
  4. Choose Load unpacked.
  5. Select the local extension folder from this project.

Capture flow

The extension captures the visible tab, crops only your selected rectangle, uploads it to Eyentra, then opens the QR/password detail page automatically.
